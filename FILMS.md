# Cat Cartoons: film checklist

Every film is 5:55, starring Whiskers and Luna (with Pip). Finished films are saved in
`catcute/<Place>/<Title>.mp4` on GitHub and in `~/Desktop/catcute/<Place>/` when rendered on a Mac.

**Rule:** check this list before making a new film. A city marked `[x]` is done, so don't make it
again. When a new film is finished, tick it here and fill in its title and folder.
